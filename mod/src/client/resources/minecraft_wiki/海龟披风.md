# 海龟披风

*页面ID: 186293* | *来源: Minecraft Wiki*

* *海龟披风（Turtle Cape）**是一件个人披风。

# 描述
billyK_因建议在游戏中加入[海龟](海龟.md)而被给予该披风。<ref>[Turtle shoutout to billyK_! As suspected, there's a cape waiting for you on your Minecraft profile =)](https://www.reddit.com/r/Minecraft/comments/7veimo/turtle_shoutout_to_billyk_as_suspected_theres_a/)</ref>
该披风又因为端点测试而被给予了Mojang雇员[Michael Andersson](https://namemc.com/profile/fe5d6f18-6b24-4afb-8fba-fd8768d96e4b)和[el_hefe_](https://namemc.com/profile/e9cd1af9-6152-40d7-b957-4969094e077c)，但el_hefe_的披风之后被移除了。
